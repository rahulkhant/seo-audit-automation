H3 Step 1: Create A Google Merchant Center Account 127 words
Go to merchants.google.com and sign in with an existing Google/Gmail account (or create one). Click 'Get started', accept the Merchant Center Terms of Service, and select the business's country and time zone -- note that country selection affects which policies and tax rules apply later.

H3 Follow Shopping Ads policies 126 words
Adhere to Shopping ads policies specifically: accurate pricing and availability that match the landing page, working links, and meeting editorial/content requirements for product data.
H3 Avoid prohibited practices 126 words
Cover the common violations: misleading claims, counterfeit goods, clickbait product titles, and keyword stuffing in titles/descriptions. Note these are frequent, avoidable causes of suspension.
H3 Don't sell restricted products (or follow restrictions) 126 words
Explain that certain categories are banned or restricted (weapons, tobacco, counterfeit goods, certain health claims) and merchants should check Google's restricted products list before listing anything in a gray-area category.
H3 Only promote products available for direct purchase 126 words
Landing pages must let a user actually buy the exact item shown in the ad/listing -- no bait-and-switch, no listing out-of-stock items without correctly marking availability status.
H3 Use a supported language consistently 126 words
Product titles and descriptions must be in a language Google supports for the target country, and must stay consistent with the account's configured target country/language settings.
H3 Collect user information securely 126 words
Checkout and any data collection must run over HTTPS/SSL, with a clear privacy policy and secure payment processing -- Google checks for this as part of trustworthiness requirements.
H3 Sign in regularly 126 words
Google expects periodic account activity; extended inactivity can lead to an account being flagged or suspended automatically, so regular sign-ins matter even once everything is running smoothly.
